# EU AI Act: what applies when

**Verified 16 August 2026** against the consolidated text, [CELEX 02024R1689-20260727](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02024R1689-20260727).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Digital Omnibus on AI was adopted.** It is not a proposal. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 was published in the OJ on 24 July 2026 and entered into force on **27 July 2026**. It amends the AI Act in 43 places, including the high-risk application dates. A large amount of published guidance, including timelines that are still live on well-known AI Act sites, has not been updated. If a source shows Annex III high-risk applying in August 2026, it predates the Omnibus.

## Applicability, from Article 113

| Date | What starts applying |
|---|---|
| **2 Feb 2025** | Chapters I and II: definitions, **AI literacy (Art. 4)**, **prohibited practices (Art. 5)** |
| **2 Aug 2025** | Notified bodies (Art. 28 to 39), **GPAI models (Art. 51 to 56)**, governance (Art. 64 to 70), **penalties (Art. 99 to 100)**, confidentiality (Art. 78) |
| **2 Aug 2026** | **General application.** Standards and conformity assessment machinery (Art. 40 to 49), **Art. 50 transparency**, sandboxes, the EU database (Art. 71), **post-market monitoring, serious incidents and market surveillance (Art. 72 to 94)**, GPAI fines (Art. 101) |
| **2 Dec 2026** | New Art. 5 prohibitions on non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM. Also Art. 111(4): generative systems already on the market must meet Art. 50(2) machine-readable marking |
| **2 Aug 2027** | GPAI models placed on the market before 2 Aug 2025 must be brought into compliance (Art. 111(3)). National sandboxes operational (Art. 57(1), moved by the Omnibus) |
| **2 Dec 2027** | **Annex III high-risk.** Chapter III Sections 1 to 3: the Art. 8 to 15 requirements, and the Art. 16 to 27 provider and deployer obligations, including the **Art. 27 FRIA** |
| **2 Aug 2028** | **Annex I high-risk**, meaning AI that is, or is a safety component of, a regulated product |
| **2 Aug 2030** | Legacy high-risk systems **intended for use by public authorities** (Art. 111(2)) |
| **31 Dec 2030** | AI components of Annex X large-scale EU IT systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2027 |

## Three things people get wrong

**1. The Act was not delayed.** Only Chapter III Sections 1 to 3 moved. Prohibitions, GPAI obligations, Article 50 transparency, market surveillance, the EU database and penalties all run on the original schedule. Article 50 has been enforceable since 2 August 2026, backed by fines of up to 15 million euro or 3 percent of worldwide turnover.

**2. The FRIA is deferred to 2 December 2027**, not August 2026. Article 27 sits in Chapter III Section 3. Guides that say otherwise have not been updated.

**3. AI literacy was softened, not removed.** The November 2025 proposal would have deleted the operator obligation. The enacted text keeps Article 4, requiring providers and deployers to take measures to support AI literacy, while stating expressly that it "does not require providers or deployers to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual."

## One open question worth knowing about

Article 113(3)(c) defers **Sections 1, 2 and 3** of Chapter III. Article 43 (conformity assessment) and Article 49 (registration) sit in **Section 5**, which is not named in the deferral, so on the face of the text they apply from 2 August 2026. In practice the provider-facing triggers run through Article 16(f) and 16(i), which are deferred, so the deferral very likely carries them.

The enacted text does not say so. Treat this as unresolved and take advice rather than assuming either reading.

## What is still missing

Plan around these gaps rather than waiting for them.

| Instrument | Status as of 16 Aug 2026 |
|---|---|
| Art. 6(5) high-risk classification guidelines | **Draft only.** Published 19 May 2026, consultation closed 23 July 2026. The statutory deadline of 2 Feb 2026 was missed |
| Art. 27(5) FRIA template | **Not published.** Build your own against Art. 27(1)(a) to (f) |
| Art. 72(3) post-market monitoring template | Due 2 September 2027 |
| **Harmonised standards cited in the OJ** | **None.** No AI system can currently claim the Article 40 presumption of conformity |

On that last point: EN 18286:2026, the European Standard for a quality management system for AI Act purposes, was published in 2026, and the Commission is expected to cite it later in the year. Until a reference appears in the Official Journal, the Article 40 presumption is not available to anyone. For Annex III point 1 systems, where Annex VII applies when no harmonised standard has been used, plan for notified body involvement and treat it as a twelve to eighteen month path.
